PaperConnekt icon

Custom vector artwork for PaperConnekt: a folded-paper P beside a separate
three-node research graph. This replaces the initial Material chain-link
concept and does not contain its glyph.

The P represents papers, the folded corner reinforces the document metaphor,
and the connected nodes represent relationships between research ideas.
The blue gradient uses #3B82F6, #2457A6, and #173D80.

Platform exports include Android adaptive and monochrome layers, desktop
packaging icons, and theme-specific plugin variants.
